Kaitlyn's Quest 5.0.0

This release rebuilds her app on Ty's latest engine (7.1.0). Her saved progress, equipment and
settings carry over unchanged: the save key is the same one her app already uses.

What is new for her (all of it from Ty's current app)
- Today tab: Main quests first, then the daily commitments and quest board; the optional
  adventures and boss are tucked into one collapsible section.
- A "How is your capacity today?" check that scores nothing.
- Carryover help: after three rollovers an item offers Make smaller, Reschedule or Release.
- Weigh-in: a prompt from 8 in the morning when five days have passed since the last entry.
  The log lives in Extras.
- Busy Day has no weekly limit.
- Repeating tasks can be stopped without deleting the current one.
- "Already done" when logging something that is already finished.
- Streak milestones at 30, 60, 90, 180 and 365 days.
- Vitality has a tap-to-read explanation.
- A daily scripture on the Hero tab.
- Together tab: the shared calendar with Ty.
- A fix that makes every penalty respect the same daily loss cap (the sixth seasonal
  commitment used to take XP outside it).
- Many more outfits, weapons and two more companions (owlbear, phoenix).

What stays hers
- Her attributes, quests, bosses, seasons, skills, companions (Ty and Lawson) and birthdays.
- Burn Boot Camp: logging a session now also completes the daily movement commitment.
- Her title map and theme song, her hero scenes (6am and 6pm), her parchment background.
- Weapon angles: sword, axe, hammer and paddle at -100 degrees, staff and spear at 100 degrees
  (other blade and pole weapons follow the same rule).
- No church check-in, no headwear or off-hand slots.
